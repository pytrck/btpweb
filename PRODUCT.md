# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Czech consumers and small businesses with two jobs to be done: "my phone/laptop is broken and I don't want to be ripped off" and "I need a website that actually earns, not a template." They arrive skeptical — burned by slow repair shops, vague pricing, and page-builder agencies. Within seconds they're judging: are these people competent, honest, and worth contacting? The site's single conversion goal is an inquiry (contact form / direct channels).

## Product Purpose

Break The Pattern (BTP) is an indie tech team: phone & hardware repair, custom web development, diagnostics, data recovery, system optimization. This site is the client-acquisition surface — the landing page IS the portfolio. Success = qualified inquiries. Because BTP sells "no templates, craft first," the site itself must be walking proof: if the site feels generic, the promise collapses.

## Brand Personality

Precise, defiant, honest. "Break the pattern" = refusal of average — slow service, template sites, price fog. Premium through craft and control, not luxury clichés. Emotional goals: quiet confidence, technical edge, a little danger (the fracture), zero sales sleaze. Voice is direct Czech, anti-corporate ("Bez prodejních keců").

## Anti-references

- Template agency sites and page-builder slop (Wix/Squarespace genericism) — the exact thing BTP breaks.
- Local repair-shop sites: cluttered, stocky, discount-banner energy.
- Apple-clone minimalism: glossy/glassy Apple-grade *quality* is the bar, but copying Apple's look is failure — BTP is darker, sharper, fractured.
- Generic AI-landing-page grammar: interchangeable hero, identical card grids, hype metrics.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof.** Every detail (motion, hairlines, focus states) demonstrates the craft being sold. Anything that looks templated contradicts the product.
2. **One signature, many echoes.** The fracture — seam, glint, vapor crack, split type — is THE motif. New moments should echo it, not introduce competing effects.
3. **Depth from light on ink.** Premium = atmosphere (glow, grain, glass, spotlight) on the dark surface — never decoration for its own sake. Sharp 2px radii stay; BTP is not soft.
4. **Honest conversion.** Concrete promises (24h reply, price upfront, warranty) do the selling. No hype, no fake urgency, no inflated metrics.
5. **Motion earns its place.** One-shot signatures (hero strike), calm scroll reveals, tactile hovers. Everything has a reduced-motion fallback and real text stays in the DOM.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1 on ink, visible keyboard focus (`btp-focus` vapor outline), `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks everywhere (static fractured state, no strikes/pulses), semantic HTML with screen-reader text for decorative type effects, Czech + English locales.
